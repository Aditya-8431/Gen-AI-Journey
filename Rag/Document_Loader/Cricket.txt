The morning sun begins to rise and bake the hardened clay,
A canvas rolled and painted white to welcome in the day.
The wooden stumps are rooted deep, a kingdom to defend,
Where heroes rise and legends fall, and rivalries transcend.

The leather ball is gleaming red, its seam a perfect crest,
Waiting for the seamer’s grip to put it to the test.
The umpire flips a silver coin that tumbles in the air,
A silent prayer escapes the lips of captains standing there.

The fielding side fans out in green, a predatory ring,
Anticipating every edge and what the pitch might bring.
The opening batter taps his bat, a rhythmic, steady beat,
**His focus narrowing to the space where bat and leather meet.**

A hush descends upon the stands, a breath collectively held,
As down the track the bowler runs, by unseen force propelled.
He leaps into the heavy breeze, his arm a whipping arc,
To hurl a blazing thunderbolt and leave a fiery mark.

The willow flashes in the sun, a crisp and sounding crack,
The ball goes racing past the point, there is no turning back.
The crowd erupts in sudden joy, a tidal wave of sound,
As scarlet rushes to the ropes across the sunlit ground.

But now the shadows lengthen out, the spinners take the stage,
To weave a web of subtle flight and turn the turning page.
A loop, a drift, a vicious spin, a sudden, sharp defeat,
The batter shakes a weary head and sounds a slow retreat.

The wicket-keeper crouches low, a hawk with watchful eyes,
His gloves a pair of velvet traps to catch a quick surprise.
A whispered nick, a sudden shout, an index finger high,
The batter slowly walks away beneath the fading sky.

It is a game of patience now, of blocks and careful leaves,
Of building up a partnership while dusty daylight weaves.
The middle order digs its heels against the rising tide,
With every stolen single cheered by those who watch with pride.

The afternoon brings weary legs and sweat upon the brow,
But deep inside the burning chest, they make a silent vow.
To chase the total, run by run, until the dying light,
To keep the roaring flame of hope burning through the night.

The fast men return to the crease with fire in their stare,
They polish up the scuffed red ball and breathe the humid air.
A bouncer climbs toward the throat, a vicious, rising beast,
A hook shot sends it in the stands, a sudden, thrilling feast.

**The mathematics of the chase begin to grip the mind,**
With fifty needed off the last, and wickets hard to find.
The tension hangs a heavy mist upon the outfield grass,
As golden hours turn to bronze and fleeting moments pass.

The floodlights pierce the gathering dusk with artificial beams,
Illuminating diving saves and desperate, fading dreams.
A run-out sparks a frantic yell, the flashing bails alight,
A millimeter’s difference to decide the epic fight.

The final over comes at last, a silence fills the stands,
The destiny of nations held within a bowler’s hands.
Six runs required off a ball, the final act to play,
The universe reduces down to this one frantic fray.

He charges in with everything his weary legs can yield,
The ball pitched up, the willow swings with power unrevealed.
It soars above the boundary rope, a comet in the night,
And thousands leap into the air, consumed by pure delight.

The pitch will sleep in silence soon, the stumps will be undone,
But memories of the battle fought will echo in the sun.
For this is more than bat and ball upon the sunlit green,
It is the greatest, noblest dance the sporting world has seen.
